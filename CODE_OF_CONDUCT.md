# Code of conduct

SameByte welcomes contributors and users regardless of background, experience,
identity, or personal circumstances. This policy applies to issues, pull
requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and when representing the project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Discuss code, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than
making personal judgments. Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and
allow people to participate without harassment or pressure to disclose personal
information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Neither are
spam or deliberate attempts to derail a discussion after a maintainer asks for
it to stop.

## Reporting a concern

The project maintainer, [0then0](https://github.com/0then0), handles reports.
For behavior already visible in a public thread, you may ask the maintainer to
review that thread. Do not repeat abusive content or publish private information.

For a confidential concern, open a minimal issue asking the maintainer to
arrange a private contact channel. Do not include allegations, identities, or
sensitive details in that issue. Share the details only once a private channel
has been agreed. The project does not currently advertise a dedicated private
conduct-reporting inbox.

## Enforcement

The maintainer considers the context, impact, and available evidence, and may
request clarification from the people involved. Actions can include editing or
removing content, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban for
serious or repeated violations. Participants must respect requests to stop
unacceptable behavior.

Reports are shared only as needed to investigate and enforce this policy.
Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, particularly for content already
public or incidents handled by GitHub. You can also report platform abuse
through [GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines).
